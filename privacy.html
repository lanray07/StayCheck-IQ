Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 21, 2026

StayCheck IQ is designed for Airbnb and short-let turnover inspections. This policy explains the data handled by the app.

Data You Add

The app may store property details, checklist notes, room photos, issue descriptions, inventory quantities, report summaries, and PDF reports. In the current mock build, this information is stored locally on your device using Apple SwiftData.

AI Processing

Mock AI mode is enabled by default. Production remote AI scanning should send inspection data only to a secure backend controlled by StayCheck IQ. API keys must not be stored in the iOS app.

Subscriptions

Purchases and subscriptions are handled by Apple through StoreKit. StayCheck IQ receives subscription entitlement status from Apple to unlock paid features.

Use of Data

Inspection data is used to provide app functionality such as turnover checklists, photo proof, issue reporting, inventory tracking, guest-ready scoring, and PDF report generation. It is not used for third-party advertising.
